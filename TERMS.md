# Simple usage terms

- Use only accounts and sites you own or are authorized to manage.
- Publish content and images you own or may reuse. Follow each destination's rules, attribution requirements and rate limits.
- Review before publishing: articles and uploaded photos can become public. Website deployment may replace the selected site's existing files.
- Free plans have limits. The app cannot guarantee traffic, ranking, moderation approval or continued availability of third-party APIs.
- Keep passwords, tokens and local backups private. Change the initial admin password before giving anyone network access.
- Removing a local draft does not remove an external publication. Use the destination's own editor to update or delete it.
- Recorded view counts and manually supplied URLs may be incomplete. Only results verified with a real account can establish that account's publishing readiness.
- The software is supplied as-is. You are responsible for reviewing results and maintaining backups.
